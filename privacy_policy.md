# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 2026

**Last Updated:** June 23, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Our application is designed with a privacy-first approach. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

**Core Principle:** This app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. All processing is performed locally on your device. No internet connection is required unless explicitly stated in the app's features.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

### We Do NOT Collect:
- Your name, email, phone number, or any account information
- Financial data you enter (investment amounts, income, loan details)
- Location data
- Device identifiers (IMEI, Advertising ID, etc.)
- Contact lists or device content
- Browsing history or usage analytics

### What Stays on Your Device:
- **Calculation history** — your last 20 calculations (timestamps, inputs, results)
- **App preferences** — your chosen language, theme, number format
- **Feature settings** — which beta features you have enabled

All this data is stored locally in your device's private app storage. Nothing is sent to our servers or any third party.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage

Your calculations and settings are saved in:
- **DataStore (Preferences)** — encrypted by Android's Keystore
- **Private app cache** — not accessible to other apps

You can clear all data by:
1. Android Settings → Apps → Labh Mantra
2. Tap "Storage & cache" → "Clear storage"

All your calculation history and settings will be permanently deleted.

---

## 4. Crash Reporting (Optional)

If the app crashes, we detect this on the next launch and offer you the option to share a diagnostic report **via your device's share sheet** (email, WhatsApp, etc.). This is strictly **opt-in**.

The crash report contains only:
- Timestamp of the crash
- App version number
- Android OS version and SDK level
- Device manufacturer and model (e.g., "Samsung Galaxy A52")
- Technical stack trace showing where the error occurred

**What the report does NOT contain:**
- Any calculation inputs or financial figures you entered
- Personally identifiable information
- Passwords or sensitive data
- Your contacts or files

You can inspect the full report text before sending. If you decline, the report is permanently deleted from your device.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

### Firebase Crashlytics (Future Addition)
In future versions, we may use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports to help us fix bugs faster. If implemented:
- You will be notified before this feature is enabled
- The same data restrictions apply (no PII, no calculation inputs)
- Crash data will be encrypted in transit
- You can opt out in app settings

### Google Play Store
Your app download, installation, and update history is managed by Google Play. Please review [Google Play's Privacy Policy](https://play.google.com/intl/en/about/play-core/privacy-security/) for details.

---

## 6. No Third-Party Ad Networks or Analytics

This app does **not** use:
- Google Analytics
- Firebase Analytics
- AdMob
- Any ad networks
- Any user tracking libraries

---

## 7. Data Security

- **Encryption at rest:** App preferences are encrypted using Android's Keystore
- **Encryption in transit:** If crash reporting is ever enabled, data is sent over HTTPS
- **No third-party access:** Your device data is never shared with anyone outside your device
- **No cloud backup of sensitive data:** Calculation history is not synced to cloud

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has used the app, please contact us to request data deletion (though no personal data is collected).

---

## 9. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access:** View all data stored by the app by opening your device settings
- **Deletion:** Clear all app data instantly (Settings → Apps → Labh Mantra → Storage & cache → Clear storage)
- **Control:** Choose which features to enable (language, theme, beta features)
- **No selling:** Your data will never be sold to anyone
